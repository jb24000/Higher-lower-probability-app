Higher / Lower Probability Predictor

Enter the last 5 outcomes and this app estimates whether the next outcome is more likely to be higher or lower than either the rolling average or a custom threshold. Confidence is driven primarily by trend consistency, then adjusted by how far the projected next value is from the selected target.

How the prediction works

This version uses a rules-based model. It first projects a likely next value using slope and momentum. Then it compares that projected value to the selected target. The final confidence is mostly based on trend consistency and partly on the projected separation from the target.
