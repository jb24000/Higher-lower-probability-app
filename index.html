Higher / Lower Probability Predictor

Paste your last 20 outcomes from oldest to newest. The app projects the next number, puts the most weight on the newest values, blends in the current season average, builds a hybrid volatility band that works better across both low-count and higher-count stats, and compares the projected next number to your custom threshold.

How to read the result

The main answer is the projected next number. The app then shows the volatility as an error band around that projection.

Example: if the projected next number is 10.0 and volatility is ±0.5, the expected range is 9.5 to 10.5. The app compares the projected next number to your custom threshold and labels it HIGHER or LOWER, plus WEAK or STRONG.
